## Recipe Overview
This Quick Avocado Toast with Eggs recipe is an uncomplicated yet delicious way to start your day. Within just 10 minutes, you can whip up two servings of satisfying and healthy content. Perfectly toasted whole grain bread serves as a sturdy base for creamy mashed avocado and a vibrant egg, making it a delightful breakfast that appeals to a variety of taste buds. 

- Prep Time: 5 minutes
- Cook Time: 5 minutes
- Total Time: 10 minutes
- Course: Breakfast
- Cuisine: American
- Servings: 2
- Calories: ~400 per serving

## Ingredients
A simple yet nourishing selection of ingredients makes this recipe accessible and delightful.

### Core Ingredients
- 2 slices whole grain bread
- 1 ripe avocado
- 2 large eggs

### Seasoning & Extras
- Salt and pepper: To taste
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- Optional: Red pepper flakes and lemon juice

## Instructions
1. Toast the whole grain bread slices until golden brown.
2. While the bread is toasting, heat olive oil in a non-stick skillet over medium heat.
3. Crack the eggs into the skillet and cook to your desired doneness (sunny-side up or over-easy).
4. Meanwhile, cut the avocado in half, remove the pit, and scoop the flesh into a bowl.
5. Mash the avocado with a fork and season with salt, pepper, and optional lemon juice.
6. Spread the mashed avocado generously on each slice of toasted bread.
7. Once the eggs are cooked, place one egg on top of each slice of avocado toast.
8. Sprinkle with red pepper flakes if desired and serve immediately.

## Cooking Essentials
Before diving into the cooking process, ensure you have the right tools handy.

### Kitchen Tools Required
- Non-stick skillet: Essential for cooking the eggs without sticking. Using a non-stick skillet ensures that you won’t have to struggle with stuck-on egg or any frustrating clean-up.
- Toaster: For achieving that golden-brown toast. A good toaster will give you even browning, enhancing both the texture and taste of the bread.
- Mixing bowl: To mash the avocado. A sturdy mixing bowl ensures that you can effectively mash and season the avocado without any mess.

### Recommended Techniques
- Toasting: Utilize a toaster for an even, crisp toast. If you wish, you can also use a skillet, though a toaster is often more efficient for producing consistently golden slices.
- Skillet Cooking: Learn how to manage heat for perfectly cooked eggs. The heat should be moderate; if the skillet is too hot, the eggs may cook too quickly, while a cooler pan might lead to unevenly cooked eggs. Aim for a consistent sizzle that will ensure they cook through without burning.

The beauty of this Quick Avocado Toast with Eggs lies in its straightforward approach to cooking. By familiarizing yourself with these fundamental techniques and tools, you can effortlessly create a breakfast staple that nourishes both the body and the soul.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

Following the steps closely will ensure a flavorful outcome.

### Preparing the Bread

To begin your Quick Avocado Toast with Eggs, the first thing you'll need to do is toast the whole grain bread slices. Utilizing a toaster or an oven, toast the bread until it achieves a golden brown color. This initial step is crucial, as it allows the natural flavors of the bread to develop, which will complement the other ingredients beautifully. Whole grain bread not only adds a chewy texture but also provides essential fiber, making this breakfast option both delicious and nutritious.

### Cooking the Eggs

While the bread is toasting, it's time to focus on the eggs. Heat a tablespoon of olive oil in a non-stick skillet over medium heat. This oil will not only prevent the eggs from sticking but also impart a rich flavor. Once heated, crack two large eggs into the skillet, ensuring that they don't break. You have the option to cook them according to your preference: sunny-side up, where the yolk remains runny, or over-easy, where it is gently flipped, allowing the whites to fully set while keeping the yolk partially runny. Cooking to your desired doneness gives you the control necessary for ideal texture and flavor.

### Mashing the Avocado

Next, turn your attention to the avocado, which is the star of this dish. Start by cutting the ripe avocado in half and carefully removing the pit. Use a spoon to scoop the creamy flesh into a bowl. The freshness and richness of the avocado are essential to creating a satisfying toast. Use a fork to mash the avocado until you achieve your desired consistency—smooth or chunky, whichever you prefer. Season the mashed avocado with a sprinkle of salt and pepper for taste. If you're feeling adventurous, add a dash of fresh lemon juice. This not only elevates the flavor but also adds a hint of brightness that cuts through the richness of the avocado.

### Assembling the Toast

Once the toast has reached that perfect golden brown color and the eggs are cooked just right, it's time to assemble your dish. Take each slice of toasted bread and spread the generous amount of mashed avocado on top. Use the back of a spoon or a knife to create an even layer, ensuring that every bite is coated in this creamy goodness. Then, gently place one egg atop each slice of avocado toast. As a final touch, consider sprinkling red pepper flakes over the eggs for a kick of flavor. Serve immediately to enjoy the delightful combination of textures and flavors.

## Meal Prep and Storage

While this dish is best enjoyed fresh, understanding how to store leftover ingredients can be beneficial.

### Storing Avocado

If you have any leftover avocado, it’s important to store it properly to maintain its freshness. The best way to prevent the avocado from browning is to place it in an airtight container. An added tip is to drizzle a little lemon juice over the cut surface before sealing. The acidity in the lemon juice can help preserve the vibrant green color and rich flavor of the avocado.

### Preparing in Advance

While the entire meal is designed to be prepared quickly, you can streamline the process by preparing certain elements in advance. Mash the avocado ahead of time and store it using the aforementioned storage techniques to keep it fresh. You can also pre-cook the eggs if you’re in a hurry—just make sure to reheat them gently to maintain their texture. This way, you can assemble your Quick Avocado Toast even faster when hunger strikes.
